WARRANTY POLICY FOR MACBOOK PRO M3 MAX

1. HARDWARE COVERAGE
- Covered: Screen cracking due to spontaneous thermal stress or manufacturer defect.
- Not Covered: Screen cracking due to physical impact, dropping, or customer negligence.
- Covered: Battery swelling or failing to hold charge within 1 year.
- Covered: Keyboard malfunctioning without liquid damage.

2. CLAIM PROCESS
- Customer must provide a clear photograph or video of the damage.
- The GenLayer AI will evaluate if the damage appears to be from physical impact or a defect.

3. RESOLUTION
- If covered: 100% full refund of the warranty locked amount.
- If user negligence but partial fault (e.g., mixed cause): 50% refund.
- If totally customer fault (e.g., dropped on the floor): Claim rejected.
